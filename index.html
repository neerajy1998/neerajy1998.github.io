About

I'm a research scholar in Computer Science and Engineering at IIIT Delhi, working under Dr. Rinku Shah on a framework for in-network crypto processing built on a microservice-based architecture. The core question: can security processing move off the host and into the network itself, without slowing either down?

Before research, I studied informatics at the University of Delhi and electronics before that, and I keep one foot in teaching. I've been a teaching assistant for Introduction to Programming, Computer Networks and Data Structures & Algorithms.
