About Me

Who am I?

I am a Ph.D. research scholar in Computer Science and Engineering at the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology, Delhi (IIIT-Delhi), working under Dr. Rinku Shah on developing a framework for in-network crypto processing built on a microservice-based architecture. My work sits at the meeting point of computer networks, hardware and security: programmable data planes (P4, Intel Tofino), high-level synthesis (HLS) and microservices. Before research, I completed my M.Sc. in Informatics and B.Sc. in Electronics at the University of Delhi, and I have served three terms as a teaching assistant for programming, computer networks and data structures courses. I am a TCS Research Scholar Program (RSP) 2023 scholar, and my poster on a microservice-based in-network security framework for FPGA NICs was accepted at CCGrid 2023. Outside research, I enjoy learning new languages and I am an e-sports (CSGO) player.
